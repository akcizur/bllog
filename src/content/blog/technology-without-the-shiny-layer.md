Performance is part of the design.

A static page does most of its rendering work before the visitor arrives. That makes the experience predictable and keeps the browser workload small.

## Less runtime, more surface

Keep JavaScript where it adds meaning. Navigation, content and reading should remain ordinary HTML whenever possible.
