A good archive is a second interface.

The home page answers what is new. Categories and tags answer what is related. Together they turn a chronological feed into a useful body of work.

## Structure beats novelty

Stable routes are easier to learn than decorative navigation. Journal, category, tag and article are enough for most small editorial sites.
